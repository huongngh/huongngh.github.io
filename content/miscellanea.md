**Miscellanea** (from Latin *miscellus*, meaning "mixed"): a collection of diverse, unrelated items gathered together. 

This page is a personal collection of things that capture my attention—the strange, the beautiful, the wonderfully pointless, and the deeply thought-provoking. It's a space where I gather the internet treasures that make me pause, laugh, or think differently about the world.

Some days you need a distraction. Other days, you need something that stares back at you and makes you reconsider. This page holds both.
